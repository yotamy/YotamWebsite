Yotam Yaniv

I am a Software Engineer at Google working on YouTube.

Previously, I was the 2024 Grace Hopper fellow in computing sciences at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. I was part of the Machine Learning & Analytics Group in the Scientific Data Division, focusing on randomized numerical linear algebra for high-performance computing and machine learning for science.

Before that, I received my PhD in applied mathematics from the University of California, Los Angeles. My advisors were Professor Deanna Needell and Professor Andrea Bertozzi. I worked on problems in stochastic iterative optimization and matrix decompositions (dissertation).

I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Maryland, College Park, graduating with a dual degree in mathematics (with high honors) and computer science (with honors). My pronouns are he/him.
